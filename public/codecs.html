This is a compilation of lossy codecs used for media. Ideal chain of codecs and containers for watching prerecorded videos: MKV container to hold the video and audio streams. AV1 video codec for the video stream. Opus audio codec for the audio stream. LC3 bluetooth codec. Ideal image format: JXL My most used nonideal image formats: HEIF JPEG What I am using right now when streaming YouTube (nonideal): AV1 or VP9 video codec for the video stream. Opus audio codec for the audio stream. APTX or AAC bluetooth codec. What I am using right now for watching self-shot iPhone 16 videos (nonideal): MOV container to hold the video and audio streams. HEVC video codec for the video stream. AAC audio codec for the audio stream. APTX or AAC bluetooth codec. Future developments: Media containers will eventually be standardized to MKV. Video streams will eventually be standardized to AV1 and its successors such as AV2. Audio streams will eventually be standardized to Opus. Bluetooth audio streams will eventually be standardized to LC3 at 345 kbps. All of the above are likely to occur due to the fact that they are all royalty free formats.
